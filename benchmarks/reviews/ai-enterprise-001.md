# Benchmark Review: Set Sail Enterprise AI

## Scope

This public-only case uses the official [Google Cloud customer story](https://cloud.google.com/customers/set-sail). It tests whether a public enterprise deployment and customer signal remain separate from startup-program eligibility and a current funding need.

## Confirmed public facts

- Set Sail is described as a Hong Kong enterprise SaaS provider.
- Its product applies AI and natural-language processing to customer-service and sales workflows.
- The official customer story records a deployed Google Cloud workload and public enterprise/SMB customer signal.

## Facts retained as unknown

- Current revenue, funding need, runway, legal-entity details, and fundraising stage.
- AWS, Microsoft, NVIDIA, OpenAI, or YC program eligibility and affiliations.
- Prior cloud credits, application history, and a named program access path.

## Expected decision boundaries

A public deployment proves more than a product description, but it does not prove eligibility for unrelated providers or establish a current application intent.

## Quality signal under test

The engine must retain the deployment signal while refusing to transform a vendor customer story into universal program eligibility.
