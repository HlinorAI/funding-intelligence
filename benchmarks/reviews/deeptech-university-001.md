# Benchmark Review: Quantum Motion University Deeptech

## Scope

This public-only case uses UCL's official [funding and deployment report](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2026/may/quantum-computing-spinout-raises-ps120-million) and [quantum spinout directory](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/quantum/innovation/startups-and-spinouts). It tests a university-linked hardware project outside the repository's main AI and Web3 coverage.

## Confirmed public facts

- Quantum Motion is a UCL- and Oxford-linked spinout developing silicon quantum computers.
- UCL records a public funding round and a commercial deployment at the UK National Quantum Computing Centre.
- The product is deeptech hardware intended for data-centre deployment.

## Facts retained as unknown

- Current revenue, runway, funding need, and application intent.
- Eligibility or affiliation with the programs represented in the local AI pack.
- A native NVIDIA dependency or GPU workload.
- Provider-account details, prior credits, and accelerator application history.

## Expected decision boundaries

The public funding and deployment signals must not create an application recommendation. The normal runner should report a knowledge-coverage gap because the project is outside the AI/Web3 loading boundary. When an AI-pack route is explicitly inspected, deeptech category overlap alone remains insufficient without provider eligibility, current need, and native workload facts.

## Quality signal under test

The case measures whether the bounded knowledge snapshot produces conservative decisions for a credible project shape outside its primary coverage instead of inventing a specialized funding route.
