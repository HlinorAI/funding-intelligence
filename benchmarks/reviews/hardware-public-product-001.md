# Benchmark Review: Almond Public Product

## Scope

This is a public-only hardware and physical-AI benchmark. It uses the public [YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/almond-2) and the [Axol product site](https://www.almond.bot/). It is not founder feedback and does not establish funding outcomes.

## Confirmed public facts

- Almond is an active YC Spring 2025 company.
- Axol is a publicly described dual-arm robot for physical-AI work.
- The product site presents public hardware specifications, a demo, an order path, and an open-source SDK.

## Facts retained as unknown

- Funding stage, legal entity details, revenue, customers, users, pilots, and partnerships.
- Current production volume and current deployed-robot count.
- Cloud-provider eligibility, prior cloud credits, and a specific NVIDIA or CUDA workload.

## Expected decision boundaries

| Route | Expected boundary | Review rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Y Combinator | `DO_NOT_APPLY` | The public profile establishes a current affiliation, which overrides fit. |
| AWS Activate | `COMPLETE_ELIGIBILITY_DATA` | A public product and SDK do not prove provider eligibility, account state, credit history, or intended workload. |
| Microsoft for Startups | `COMPLETE_ELIGIBILITY_DATA` | The public sources do not establish the company and Azure eligibility facts required for access. |
| NVIDIA Inception | `BUILD_NVIDIA_USE_CASE` | Physical AI alone is not proof of a documented NVIDIA-specific compute or deployment need. |
| OpenAI for Startups | `VERIFY_ACCESS_PATH` | The public sources do not show a current program access path. |

## Quality signal under test

The runner must distinguish a publicly demonstrable hardware product from the separate, unproven facts required for startup benefits. It must also block a duplicate YC application despite strong category fit.
