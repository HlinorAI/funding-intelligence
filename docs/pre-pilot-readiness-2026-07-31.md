**Review date:** 2026-07-31  
**Scope:** canonical public workflow, pilot measurement contract, and the five recorded application endpoints.

## Benchmark readiness

- Seven public-only decision-quality cases are complete.
- The three new shapes are open-source AI infrastructure, enterprise AI with a public deployment signal, and university-linked deeptech.
- Public-only benchmarks remain regression evidence and are not counted as owner validation.
- Three planned shapes remain before the ten-case breadth target.

## Pilot measurement contract

The fixed feedback form records:

- classification accuracy;
- useful routes, false positives, and false negatives;
- unsupported facts or promoted unknowns;
- manual intake corrections;
- owner and operator time;
- questions requiring explanation;
- estimated time saved and repeat-use intent.

The interview order is fixed before the first owner review so questions are not adapted to defend the generated report.

## No-inbound decision

If no consented project arrives within 21 days of an intake-invitation refresh:

1. verify the intake and Discussion links;
2. review invitation clarity, privacy wording, and time commitment;
3. place the public sample report where the target audience can evaluate it;
4. review distribution to relevant founder and maintainer communities;
5. do not infer a need for new engine features from the lack of inbound cases.

## Readiness conclusion

No new product layer is required before the first pilot. The remaining prerequisite is a successful clean dry run of the documented intake-to-report workflow.
